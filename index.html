Privacy Policy

Photobe · com.aariyan.photoeditor
Effective 4 September 2026 · Last updated 4 September 2026

The short version. Photobe edits your photos entirely on your phone. Your photos and the edits you make to them are never uploaded to us or to anyone else, and they are never used to train anything.

What we do collect is anonymous usage and crash information, so we can tell which tools people use and fix the app when it breaks. There are no adverts in Photobe, and we do not sell or share your data.

1. Who we are

Photobe is published on Google Play by Agonized Soul ("we", "us"). This policy covers the Photobe Android application, package name com.aariyan.photoeditor, and nothing else.

For any question about this policy, or to make a request about your data, email abdulhady.aariyan@gmail.com.

2. Your photos

This is the part most people want answered, so it comes first.

  • Every edit — background removal, blur, retouching, filters, text, collage, and all the rest — is computed on your device.
  • Your photos are not uploaded to our servers. We do not operate servers that receive images.
  • Your photos are not sent to any third party, and are not used to train any model, ours or anyone else's.
  • The background-removal and face-detection models run locally. The subject-segmentation and face-detection models are delivered to your device once by Google Play services; the multi-class segmentation model ships inside the app. After delivery, all of them run with no network connection at all.
  • Photobe reads a photo only when you choose it, and writes a photo only when you export one. Files you save stay in your device's storage under your control.

Photobe stores a small amount of information about your projects in a database on your device so you can return to your work. That database never leaves your phone and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

3. What we collect

Photobe uses Google Firebase to understand how the app is used and to find crashes. The following is the complete list.

WhatWhyCollected by
App interactions — which screens you open, which editing tool you open and apply, whether an export succeeded or failed, whether onboarding was completed To see which tools people actually use, and where the app is confusing or failing Firebase Analytics
Crash logs and diagnostics — stack traces, the app version, the Android version, the device model, and the state of the device when a crash happened To find and fix crashes Firebase Crashlytics
A device identifier — a randomly generated app-instance ID created by Firebase for this installation of Photobe To count sessions and users without knowing who you are Firebase
Purchase events — the product identifier, amount and currency of a subscription or donation you complete, and whether a purchase was started, cancelled or failed To understand which options people choose and where the purchase flow breaks Firebase Analytics

These events carry only the categories of information listed above — for example the name of a tool such as "blur", or a size band such as "large". They never carry your photos, filenames, or the contents of anything you edit.

4. What we do not collect

  • Your photos or videos.
  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any account. Photobe has no sign-in.
  • Your advertising ID. Photobe contains no advertising, no ad networks, and no attribution SDKs, and it does not read the Android advertising identifier.
  • Your location.
  • Your contacts, messages, calendar, call logs, health data, or microphone.
  • Files other than the photo you explicitly choose.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising or any other purpose.

5. Third-party services

Photobe relies on the following Google services. Each is governed by Google's own privacy policy, at policies.google.com/privacy.

  • Firebase Analytics — the usage events described in section 3.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash reports and diagnostics.
  • Google Play Billing — handles subscriptions and donations. Your payment details go to Google, never to us. We receive only whether a purchase is active.
  • Google Play services (ML Kit) — delivers the subject-segmentation and face-detection models to your device. Your photos are not sent anywhere as part of this; only the models travel, and they travel towards you.
  • Google Play In-App Updates — checks whether a newer version of Photobe is available.

7. Legal basis for processing (EEA and UK)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we process the data in section 3 on the basis of our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) in keeping Photobe working and in understanding which features are worth maintaining. That interest is balanced against your privacy by collecting no personal identifiers and no content. For purchases, the legal basis is performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b)).

8. Your rights, and how to delete your data

You may ask us to confirm what data relating to your installation we hold, to provide a copy of it, to correct it, or to delete it. You may also object to our processing it.

To make any of these requests, email abdulhady.aariyan@gmail.com with the subject line "Data request". We will respond within 30 days. Because we hold no account and no name, please tell us the approximate dates you used the app and your device model so we can locate the records.

You can also delete everything held on your device, at any time and without asking us, by uninstalling Photobe. That removes your projects, your settings, and the local Firebase identifier. Once that identifier is gone, no future data can be linked to your past use.

9. How long we keep it

  • Analytics events — retained for up to 14 months, then deleted automatically by Google.
  • Crash reports — retained for up to 90 days.
  • Anything on your device — kept until you delete the project or uninstall the app.

10. Children

Photobe is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has used the app and you want the associated records removed, email us and we will delete them.

11. Security

All data described in section 3 is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Because your photos never leave your device, they are protected by your device's own security — your screen lock and Android's storage encryption.

12. International transfers

Firebase processes the data in section 3 on Google's infrastructure, which may be located outside your country, including in the United States. Google applies the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses to those transfers.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change what Photobe collects, we will update this page and change the "Last updated" date at the top. Material changes will also be described in the app's release notes on Google Play.
